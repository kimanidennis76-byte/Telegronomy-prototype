# Telegronomy

AI-Powered Agricultural Intelligence & Digital Farm Ecosystem

## Prototype

Version: 0.1

## Purpose

Telegronomy is an agricultural technology platform designed to connect farmers with agricultural intelligence, AI-assisted crop health assessment, agronomists, extension officers and agrovets.

## Prototype Scope

The first prototype will focus on:

- Farmer application
- Digital Farm Passport
- Farm and crop management
- Crop health assessment
- Simulated disease AI
- Initial Decision Engine
- Agronomist dashboard
- Basic administration dashboard

QR scanning, payments, advanced machine learning, satellite intelligence and IoT integrations are deferred to later phases.
